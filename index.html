About Ganesh Utsav 🐘

Ganesh Utsav is a beautiful celebration dedicated to Lord Ganesha, the remover of obstacles and the god of wisdom and prosperity.

Join us as we celebrate this divine festival with devotion, music, cultural programs, decorations, delicious prasadam and unforgettable memories.

Ganpati Bappa Morya! 🙏
